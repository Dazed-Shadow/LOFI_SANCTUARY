# Itch.io Game Jam Funnel — outreach template

**Audience:** Indie devs participating in cozy-leaning Itch.io game jams (Wholesome Direct, Cozy Spring Jam, Wholesome Snack, etc.) and post-jam devs who shipped a cozy demo in the last 90 days.

**The gift:** A free, game-safe asset bundle of lo-fi tracks pre-cleared for use in their indie project. The artist gets in-game credits + a link back to the EPK.

**The ask:** Credit in the game's credits screen + a link in the Itch.io project page footer.

---

## Template — first message (Itch.io comment or email)

```
Hey [NAME] —

Played the demo of [GAME_TITLE] yesterday. The [SPECIFIC_DETAIL: e.g. "rain in the
village scene"] hit exactly the kind of mood my [relative] writes music for.

I'm putting together a small free music bundle for cozy-genre indie devs — ~8 tracks,
WAV + MP3, all pre-cleared for in-game use (commercial or non-commercial, no royalties).
The only ask is a credit in the game's credits screen and a link in the Itch project
description. That's it.

Want me to send a download link? No pressure if it's not a fit — just thought the
vibe of [GAME_TITLE] lined up well.

— [SENDER]
```

---

## Template — game jam mass-pitch (different rules)

When pitching to a game jam announcement post (e.g. a Reddit thread or Itch jam comments), don't DM individuals — drop one comment in the public thread:

```
Hi all — for anyone working on a cozy entry this jam who needs music:

I've got a free pre-cleared lo-fi pack for jam use, made by my [relative].
8 tracks, game-safe (commercial OK, no royalties), credit-only.
Reply or DM and I'll send the link.

Not affiliated with the jam — just trying to put music in good hands.
```

This is the **one exception** to the no-mass-DM rule, because jam threads are an opt-in pool of people actively looking for assets.

---

## Template — follow-up after a game ships using the music

```
Subject: [GAME_TITLE] is out — congrats

Hey [NAME],

Saw [GAME_TITLE] dropped on Itch — congratulations, the [SPECIFIC_DETAIL] hits.

If you ever want a custom track for a future project, or a stretch goal for a
crowdfunding campaign, the artist takes commissions and the rates for indies
are friendly. No rush — just keeping the door open.

In the meantime, would you be open to a short quote about the music for the EPK?
Anything you'd want to say, doesn't have to be more than a sentence.

Cheers,
[SENDER]
[EPK_LINK]
```

---

## Anti-patterns

- ❌ Pitching tracks that aren't pre-cleared. Devs need the license certainty up-front.
- ❌ Asking for revenue share. Credit + link is the *only* ask in the funnel.
- ❌ Pitching during a jam crunch window (last 48 hours). Devs are heads-down. Pitch right after the jam ends.
- ❌ DMing developers who already have a composer credited. Check the project page first.

## Notes for Notion CRM

- `Source` field = "Itch jam: [JAM_NAME]" or "Itch project: [GAME_TITLE]"
- `Tracks Offered` = jam bundle v1 (or specific custom track)
