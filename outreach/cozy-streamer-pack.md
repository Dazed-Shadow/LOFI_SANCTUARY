# Cozy Streamer Pack — outreach template

**Audience:** Mid-tier Twitch variety streamers (200-2000 concurrents) and VTubers who play cozy games (Stardew, Animal Crossing, Coffee Talk, Unpacking, A Short Hike, Spiritfarer, etc.).

**The gift:** A free, copyright-safe "Stream Pack" — pre-cleared lo-fi tracks bundled with loopable visual overlays for their transition/intermission screens.

**The ask (eventually):** A line in panel/credits ("music by [artist] — link to EPK") and, if it lands, an ongoing relationship where new tracks land in their library first.

---

## Template — first DM (Twitch / Discord)

```
hey [NAME] — caught your [SPECIFIC_STREAM: e.g. "Stardew co-op run last Tuesday"]
and the vibe was exactly the kind of thing I'm building music for.

I've been quietly assembling a free, stream-safe lo-fi pack for cozy variety streamers —
~12 tracks pre-cleared for broadcast, plus a handful of loopable pixel-art overlays
for transition screens. No strings, no follow-required, no algorithm gimmicks.

Would you want a copy? Happy to drop a download link, and if anything fits your
stream you're free to use it however you like.

— [SENDER]
```

---

## Template — follow-up email (only if they engage)

```
Subject: Cozy Streamer Pack — [NAME] download

Hey [NAME],

Thanks for the reply earlier — here's the pack:
[LINK_TO_DROPBOX_OR_DRIVE]

Inside:

- /overlays — 6 looping pixel-art transition screens, 1080p MP4
- /credit.txt — drop-in panel/credit copy if you ever want to attribute

The clearance covers Twitch, YouTube, Kick, and Discord stages.
No expiration. No takedowns from us, ever.

If anything sticks, I'd love to hear which track — and if you ever want a custom-mood track for a specific stream theme (e.g. a chill late-night reading hour), happy to send one your way.

Cheers,
[SENDER]
[EPK_LINK]
```

---

## Anti-patterns

- ❌ Opening with "I'm a music producer" — they don't care, they get 50 of those a week.
- ❌ Long bio paragraph about the artist before the gift lands.
- ❌ Requiring a follow / sub before the download.
- ❌ Asking for anything in the first message.
- ❌ Sending the same DM to 20 streamers in one batch. The mods will flag it.

## Notes for Notion CRM

When logging a pitch in the **Outreach CRM**:
- Set `Pitch Status = Pitched` and date-stamp.
- `Tracks Offered` = "Full pack v1" (or specific track if you tailored it).
- `Follow Up Date` = +7 days. Don't message again before that.
- If they reply with anything other than a decline, flip to `Engaged` (custom status) and send the email template above.
