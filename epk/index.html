About

Just a mad bunni swimmin' overdrive in the vacuum. Most of this stuff is made in the house — lo-fi loops for cozy games that don't exist yet, the kind of soundtrack you'd hear behind a quiet farming RPG or while a stranger in a small town tells you their story. Made for streamers and indie devs who want music that takes a step back instead of pushing forward. Pre-cleared for stream and in-game use; see Licensing.
