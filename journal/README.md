# Owner Log — local journal/

**Local-only.** This entire directory is gitignored. Nothing in here is committed or published.

## Purpose

Companion to the Notion **📔 Owner Log** DB. Use either, both, or neither — whichever fits the moment:

- **Notion DB** — when you want tags, filters, mood tracking, or want to link an entry to a specific track.
- **Local markdown files** — when you want to write fast in your editor, offline, with no UI overhead.

## Conventions

- One markdown file per entry: `YYYY-MM-DD_short-slug.md`
- Front-matter is optional but useful:

```markdown
---
date: 2026-05-17
energy: medium
tags: [idea, craft]
linked_track: (track title or asset library URL)
---

# Entry title

Body...
```

- When an entry from here is worth surfacing for cross-reference (e.g. it triggered a project decision), promote it into the Notion Owner Log DB and add a one-line note here pointing to the Notion URL.

## Why both?

Local files are friction-free for raw, honest writing. The Notion DB lets you slice and pattern-match across entries later. Most thoughts live in one or the other — only the ones that matter cross over.
