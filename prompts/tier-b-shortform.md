# Tier B — Short-form socials (15-30s TikTok / Shorts / Reels)

**Pipeline:** Pika / CapCut → Alibaba WAN 2.6 or Google Flow.

**Discipline:** Use **explicit game-engine camera language**. The audience is fluent in this — "side-scrolling platformer camera," "isometric tile camera," "over-the-shoulder third-person" — and it triggers the cozy-gaming reflex.

---

## Prompt template — "fake cozy dev log" hook

```
[SHOT 1, 0-3s] [CAMERA: 2D side-scrolling platformer view, camera tracks smoothly LEFT-to-RIGHT at walking pace]
A small [CHARACTER: pixel-art character — soft hooded jacket, neutral palette] walks past a row of
[BACKGROUND: cozy wooden cottages with smoke from chimneys / cherry blossom trees in bloom / autumn leaves drifting].
Warm afternoon light, palette: sage, cream, earth brown, amber.

[SHOT 2, 3-8s] [CAMERA: zoom into a window]
Through the window: a tiny interior scene — desk with a steaming mug, a sleeping cat, rain outside.

[SHOT 3, 8-15s] [CAMERA: top-down isometric tile camera, slight rotation]
The character sits at the desk, opens a notebook. Pages flip. A houseplant sways slightly.

NO neon, NO RGB, NO sci-fi, NO photoreal.
Hand-crafted pixel art, slight imperfection.
Soft camera moves only — NO whip pans, NO shaky cam.
```

---

## Caption hooks (paste into post copy)

Confessional / world-building. First-person, soft, narrative.

- *"Designing a cozy farming RPG that doesn't exist yet, just so I have an excuse to listen to this track. Should I actually build the game?"*
- *"I made a town for a game I'll never finish. The music is by my [relative]. Reply 'town' if you'd visit."*
- *"This is the inn from chapter 2. There is no game. There is no chapter 2. There is only this music and the rain."*
- *"Three years ago this song didn't exist. Now I can't write the rest of my game without it playing."*

---

## Caption anti-patterns

- ❌ "🎵 NEW LO-FI TRACK OUT NOW!! 🎵🔥"
- ❌ Hashtag spam (`#lofi #chill #study #vibes #music`)
- ❌ "Link in bio for the full track" as the hook line
- ❌ Anything that reads as marketing-first
