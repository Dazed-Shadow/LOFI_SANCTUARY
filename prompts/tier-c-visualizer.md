# Tier C — Audio-reactive visualizers (Neural Frames)

**Pipeline:** Neural Frames (Stable Diffusion).

**Discipline:** Map shifting geometric forms or color waves to the **bass and treble bands** of the track. This tier is more abstract than A/B — it's for moments where the lo-fi music carries a stronger emotional swell that deserves a non-literal visual.

---

## Prompt template

```
An abstract audio-reactive visualizer in the Cozy Gamer Sanctuary palette
(sage green, cream, earth brown, pastel rose, amber, rain slate).

Base composition: [SHAPE: soft concentric circles / drifting hexagonal grid / undulating ribbon / blooming flower forms],
rendered with [TEXTURE: grainy paper / soft cel-shaded / hand-painted gouache].

Bass band → [LOW-FREQ REACTION: pulse the scale of the largest shape, slow breathing rhythm].
Treble band → [HIGH-FREQ REACTION: small particle bursts, leaf-fall, glitter on the edges].

Camera: slow continuous drift [DIRECTION], or locked center.
Lighting: warm key, no harsh contrast.

NO neon, NO chromatic aberration, NO Y2K aesthetic, NO eyeball-burning saturation.
Soft, warm, organic.
```
